# WASM rebuilt from ant-client #242 (`f70c39f`), compared with 0.1.4 — 2026-10-28

The bundled WASM is rebuilt from WithAutonomi/ant-client#242 (V2-1769) at
`f70c39ff08d3caa1792434d7058d32b7bf5521f2`, for 0.1.5. That PR makes a browser
start a stream's first record sooner. This branch stays a draft until #242 is
on ant-client main; `verify:wasm` enforces that.

## What the rebuild changes

ant-client [#242](https://github.com/WithAutonomi/ant-client/pull/242),
recorded in its ADR-0004 section "First record of a read (2026-10-28)":

- **A read stops asking hints that cannot hold its record.** A read's first
  hints are the client's bootstrap peers, which share few or no leading bits
  with the record. The read asked them one at a time, each over a new
  connection, while a holder that discovery had already reached waited.
  - The read now records the peers each lookup reply lists.
  - A hint with a whole close group listed nearer the record is dropped.
  - Only proven identities count: the responder, and peers with a verified
    owner-signed address record.
  - Discovery, its preconnects and the read after it are unchanged.
- **A read that continues no other fetches the record after it only once it
  has finished.** That fetch's lookup and transfer no longer compete with the
  record the read waits for, such as a video's head. Reads that continue a
  stream are unchanged.

The SDK's public API, its TypeScript and the bindings' declarations are
unchanged. The declarations are the same set in a new order, and the generated
JavaScript's closure shims are renumbered.

## Compared with 0.1.4 on mainnet

The comparison ran on try.autonomi.com's own page, with
[`stream-ab.mjs`](2026-10-28-ant-client-pr242-f70c39f/stream-ab.mjs) from try's
0.1.4 audit:
- try `cac5363` was packaged twice, differing only in the core:
  - 0.1.4's WASM (`7873c651…`);
  - this WASM (`eea59cc4…`, the same bytes this branch bundles).
- Each sample opened the page in a fresh browser context and clicked Stream on
  "Data That Encrypts Itself (with LEGO).mp4" (54.9 MB) 3 s after the page's
  client connected.
- It was timed to the first decoded frame, then played for ten seconds and was
  sought to 85%, beyond the 32 MiB read-ahead window.
- The arms rotated each round, between 15:05 and 15:26 UTC on one network, with
  the machine otherwise idle.

| engine | rounds | first frame, median | sooner | over 6 s | seek to 85%, median | sooner |
| --- | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: |
| Chromium | 20 (two runs of 10) | 5.8 → 3.1 s | 16/20 | 9 → 3 | 5.1 → 4.1 s | 14/20 |
| WebKit (Playwright) | 6 | 4.0 → 3.1 s | 5/6 | 1 → 1 | 2.9 → 2.8 s | 3/6 |

All 52 samples played, and no page reported an error.

- In Chromium, the median paired saving was 1.6 s for the first frame and 1.0 s
  for the seek. The two runs' first frames were sooner in 9 and 7 of 10.
- The WebKit seek was level: the median paired difference was 0.4 s the other
  way.
- Results: [Chromium run 1](2026-10-28-ant-client-pr242-f70c39f/stream-chromium-1.json),
  [run 2](2026-10-28-ant-client-pr242-f70c39f/stream-chromium-2.json),
  [WebKit](2026-10-28-ant-client-pr242-f70c39f/stream-webkit.json), and
  [their summaries](2026-10-28-ant-client-pr242-f70c39f/summary.txt).

ant-client #242 also traced the core's read of the video's first 1 MiB.
- **Chromium (10 rounds):** the GET to the first record's holder started 1.9 s
  after the read began, instead of 4.3 s (10/10).
- **WebKit (6 rounds):** 2.2 s instead of 4.4 s (5/6).

Through `createMediaSource()`, an earlier revision of #242 (`ea1175a`) measured
a Chromium first frame of 3.0 s against 4.4 s, in 13 of 20 rounds. That
revision also gated preconnects and counted unproven listings. This SDK's
harness was not re-run on `f70c39f`.

## Limits

- One machine and network, against live nodes whose load and versions are
  unknown; one video.
- Twenty Chromium and six WebKit rounds. Real Safari and Firefox were not
  measured.
- First reads now concentrate on the first holder found: one holder served 9
  of 10 Chromium first reads, where main's were spread over three (5, 4 and 1).
- The real-browser devnet run of earlier releases was not repeated. ant-client
  #242's CI runs its read path in real Chromium against local nodes.
